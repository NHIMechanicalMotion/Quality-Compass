# Shop-Floor Training Mock-Up: Usability Test Script & Observation Sheet

**Target System:** Phase 1a Clickable Mock-Up — Shop-Floor Operator Training & Skills Matrix  
**Test Locations:** Machining, Stamping, Robotics & Assembly Work Centers (Claremont Facilities)  
**Devices Tested:** Smartphone (iPhone), Tablet (iPad 10.9"), Shop-Floor Workstation PC  
**Session Length:** 8–10 minutes per participant  

---

## Part 1: Facilitator Script & Introduction

> *"Thanks for taking a few minutes with me. We are building a brand-new digital tool for our shop floor so you can see your training, see what machines you are qualified on, and help leads sign people off right at the machine.*  
>  
> *Today, we are testing the **screens and buttons**, not you. There are no right or wrong answers. If something looks confusing or hard to tap, that is 100% on us to fix.  
>  
> *Please think out loud as you tap through. Tell me what you are looking at and what you expect to happen when you press a button. Let’s jump in!"*

---

## Part 2: The Three Hands-On Tasks

Hand the tester the device (phone, iPad, or mouse) sitting at the **Home Screen**:

### Task 1 — Operator View (Check Training & Open a Lesson)
> **What to say to the operator:**  
> *"Pretend you just clocked in for your shift. Show me how you'd check what training you need to do next and what machines you're certified on, and then open one of your training lessons."*
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Tester taps **"Operator: View My Training & Machines"** on the home screen.
  - Tester points out the **"What Training I Need Next"** section (spots Due Soon / Expired).
  - Tester taps **"Open Training Guide"** on the Okuma Lathe lesson to view standard work steps.
  - Tester finishes without facilitator pointing or prompting.

---

### Task 2 — Supervisor Sign-Off on a Phone (Mobile Flow)
> **What to say to the supervisor / lead:**  
> *"Pretend you're a supervisor carrying your phone on the floor. You just watched Sarah Jenkins run the Haas CNC Mill safely and you want to sign her off from Level 1 (Trainee) to Level 2 (Qualified Operator). Show me how you'd find Sarah, pick that machine, and sign her off."*
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Tester taps **"Supervisor: Sign Off a Worker"**.
  - Tester locates and taps **Sarah Jenkins**.
  - Tester selects the **Haas VF-4 Vertical Mill**.
  - Tester reviews the checklist and taps **"Promote to Level 2 (Qualified Operator)"**.
  - Tester reads the **Confirmation Dialog** and taps **"Confirm & Sign Off"**.
  - Tester sees the green success message and updated badge.

---

### Task 3 — Manager Skills Matrix (Spot Overdue / Expired)
> **What to say to the supervisor or manager:**  
> *"You're getting ready for the morning standup in CNC Machining. Show me how you open the skills matrix, filter to the CNC Machining department, and spot who has a training that is expired or coming due soon."*
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Tester taps **"Manager: View Skills Matrix"**.
  - Tester taps the **"CNC Machining"** filter button.
  - Tester identifies the **Expired (Red Alert)** and **Due Soon (Amber Clock)** badges on the grid.
  - Tester taps the cell or counter card to see the expiration details.

## Part 4: Post-Test Iteration Protocol

1. If any tester hesitates for more than 5 seconds on a screen, document the exact element.
2. If any tester taps the wrong button, note the layout confusion.
3. Bring feedback directly back to the development team to update the screens until **100% of testers finish all three tasks without help**.
